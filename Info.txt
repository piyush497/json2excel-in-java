### dependency-reduced-pom.xml — what it’s for

- **Purpose**: It’s generated by the Maven Shade Plugin to produce a POM that excludes dependencies already bundled inside your shaded (fat) JAR.
- **Why**: If you `install` or `deploy` the shaded artifact to a repo, consumers shouldn’t also pull in those transitive deps (would cause duplicates/conflicts). The reduced POM lists only what’s still needed externally.
- **Contains**: Your original coordinates plus adjusted dependency list, relocations info, scopes, etc., reflecting the shaded output.
- **When you need it**: Mainly when publishing the shaded JAR to Maven repos. It avoids classpath conflicts for downstream projects.
- **Not needed to run**: It doesn’t affect running your app locally or in Docker; it’s for artifact publishing hygiene.
- **Location**: Usually generated under `target/dependency-reduced-pom.xml` during the shade phase.
